**⚔️ RAIDS & WARS: THE RULES (1/4)**
A war is a short series of raids. The only lasting prize is **land**: the exact tiles named in the declaration. Nothing else changes hands for good, and buildings are never destroyed.

**Declaring.** A leader (or a rank with the permission) names the target, a reason, and the war goal. The goal is fixed on day one and can only shrink. It is the smaller of 10% of the target's land and 20 tiles (at least 2), never the capital, never the last tile, and it must not cut their land in two.
**Notice.** The target is told at once, here and in game. The first raid is at least 24 hours later.
**Who can't be attacked.** Nations younger than 72 hours, nations on vacation shield, nations in protection or cooldown, allies, and nations that share members. A nation can have at most 2 wars at a time.
---
**⚔️ RAIDS & WARS (2/4): WHEN AND WHO**
Raids happen only in three windows, 90 minutes each, in server time (Europe/Copenhagen): **16:00-17:30, 19:00-20:30, 22:00-23:30**. Every nation picks one as its **home window**; raids on it happen only then. Change it once a week, not during a war. Use the Discord time tags to see your own local time.

**To start:** at least 3 eligible raiders and 3 eligible defenders must be online when the window opens. Eligible means: verified account, in the nation 7+ days, online *before* the window, not idle (2 minutes without input).
**No-show:** if defenders are short, the raid is cancelled and counts against them. Two defender no-shows lose the war. If raiders are short, the raid is cancelled and the attacker cannot declare for 24 hours.
**Cap:** raiders may outnumber your online, active defenders by at most 2, live. One account, one connection: a second tab is refused. No joining or leaving a nation during a war.
---
**⚔️ RAIDS & WARS (3/4): THE RAID**
Land fast, grab loot, get away before they gather.
**The zone** is the war-goal land plus an 8-tile border. **The camp** ("the ship") goes on neutral or border land at least 16 tiles from the nearest cache and cannot be destroyed. Loot is only safe once delivered, and delivery is blocked while any defender is within 3 tiles of the camp.
**Caches:** each target has 3. Raiders pick up tagged loot worth points. It can't be stored, traded or hidden, it slows you down, and carriers are marked on the map. If you fall or disconnect in a fight, you drop it for the defenders. Loot not delivered at the end returns to the caches.
**Livestock & horses:** up to 10 animals and 3 horses per raid. A horse counts once it is ridden to the camp, and goes home if the rider falls.
**Same for both sides:** 20 second respawn. Defenders never respawn within 6 tiles of a cache. You keep your gear on defeat (5% wear). Webs and water: within 4 tiles, gone after 10 seconds.
**Banned in the zone:** teleporting, flying, explosives, fire, lava, boats, damaging buildings.
---
**⚔️ RAIDS & WARS (4/4): SCORE, LAND, PEACE**
**Score:** raiders earn points for delivered loot, defeats and held horses. Defenders earn points for defeats, loot taken back and blocked deliveries. Both scores are live, and capped per raid. More points wins the raid. **Ties go to the defenders.** First to 2 raids wins the war.
**If raiders win:** they get only the named tiles. You have 72 hours to move your items; buildings stay. Losers are protected for 7 days.
**If raiders lose:** the attacker cannot declare any war for 7 days. Horses held at the camp come home with their gear.
**Surrender & peace:** either side can surrender at any time. A defender surrender gives the war-goal land at once. Peace needs both sides.
**Tribute (if enabled):** defenders may offer up to 5% of the treasury. If accepted the war ends, no land moves, and you are safe from them for 3 days.
**Fair play:** every action is logged and raids can be replayed. Dispute a raid within 24 hours with its raid ID. Staff review flags by hand; there are no automatic bans from statistics.
